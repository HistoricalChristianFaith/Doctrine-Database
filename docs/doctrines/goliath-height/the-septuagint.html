The Septuagint on How Tall Was Goliath?

Dates: 2nd c. BC (the Old Greek translation of the books after the Pentateuch, Samuel–Kings among them; the dating of individual books is tentative) · Wikipedia
Summary: The oldest translation of 1 Samuel 17:4 already gives Goliath's height as four cubits and a span — a champion of roughly 6 feet 9 inches, against the Masoretic Hebrew's "six cubits and a span" (roughly 9 feet 9 inches). In the Greek book-numbering the books of Samuel are 1–2 Kingdoms (older editions style them 1–2 Kings), so the verse falls in 1 Kingdoms 17.

Claims & evidence

Goliath's height is "four cubits and a span" (1 Kingdoms 17:4)

"And there went forth a mighty man out of the army of the Philistines, Goliath, by name, out of Geth, his height was four cubits and a span."

— The Septuagint, 1 Kings (1 Kingdoms) 17:4, trans. Sir Lancelot C. L. Brenton, source (also Brenton at eBible). Four cubits and a span comes to about 6 feet 9 inches, at about eighteen inches to the cubit.

Reception and debate

  • The "four" reading in the Greek manuscripts. It is carried by the uncials Codex Vaticanus and Codex Alexandrinus and by the Lucianic recension, from which J. Daniel Hays concludes that "four" was "very likely the original reading in the Old Greek" ("Reconsidering the Height of Goliath," JETS 48/4 (2005), 713). Clyde Billington holds instead that an Alexandrian translator converted the Hebrew's six common cubits into four Egyptian royal cubits ("Goliath and the Exodus Giants," JETS 50/3 (2007), 506–508).
  • Outliers within the Greek and recensional tradition. Not every Greek witness agrees: Symmachus and Origen's fifth column (his revised Septuagint text in the Hexapla; "Hexapla," Wikipedia) read "six," and the lone eighth-century Codex Venetus reads "five" — see J. Daniel Hays, "Reconsidering the Height of Goliath," Journal of the Evangelical Theological Society 48/4 (2005): 701–714, at 703–706, source.
  • Other witnesses to "four." The oldest extant Hebrew witness, the Dead Sea Samuel scroll 4QSama, and Josephus likewise give Goliath the four-cubit stature.
