Keil & Delitzsch on Ahaziah's age

Dates: 1872 (the Biblical Commentary on the Old Testament) · Wikipedia
Summary: The nineteenth-century commentary that set the conservative-critical consensus states the verdict flatly: the "forty-two" of 2 Chronicles 22:2 is an orthographical error for "twenty-two," located in a confusion of the Hebrew numeral-letters.

Claims & evidence

"An orthographical error for 22"

"The number 42 is an orthographical error for 22… 2 Kings 8:26."

— Keil & Delitzsch, Biblical Commentary on the Old Testament, on 2 Chronicles 22:2. They locate the slip in the Hebrew numeral-letters — "twenty" (כ, kaph) written or read as "forty" (מ, mem) — so that the corruption is mechanical, not a matter of the Chronicler holding a different figure. On their reading Kings keeps the original number and Chronicles the copyist's slip.

The same verdict from John Gill

"it seems best to acknowledge a mistake of the copier, which might easily be made through a similarity of the numeral letters, forty two, for twenty two."

— John Gill, Exposition of the Old and New Testament, on 2 Chronicles 22:2. Gill, canvassing the same options as Poole, comes down on the textual side: the numeral was miscopied. The Cambridge Bible, Barnes, and the Pulpit Commentary agree that the figure "cannot be correct" and read "twenty-two."

Notes

  • The same camp as the Goliath crux — reversed direction. Keil & Delitzsch held Chronicles the original witness in the two-slayers crux; here they hold Chronicles the corrupt one. The point is not a bias toward one book but a judgement case-by-case, and here the arithmetic decides it: the "forty-two" is impossible, so whichever book carries it carries the error.
  • The mechanism is the argument's soft spot — but not its conclusion. The received Hebrew spells the number out in words, not letter-numerals, so the exact copying mechanism (letter-numerals, as here, or the older stroke notation Archer proposes) is a reconstruction. What is not in doubt is the fact of corruption, which rests on the impossibility and the versions, not on any one theory of how the slip happened. This is weighed on the argument page.
