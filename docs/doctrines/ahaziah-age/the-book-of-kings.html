The book of Kings on Ahaziah's age

Dates: c. 560 BC (the composition of 1–2 Kings) · Wikipedia
Summary: The earliest and self-consistent witness to Ahaziah's age. 2 Kings 8:26 makes him twenty-two at his accession — the figure that agrees with the same book's notice that his father Jehoram died at forty, and the figure the ancient versions carry through even to Chronicles.

Claims & evidence

Ahaziah was twenty-two at his accession

"Two and twenty years old was Ahaziah when he began to reign; and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Athaliah, the daughter of Omri king of Israel."

— 2 Kings 8:26 (KJV), source. The verse is the parallel to 2 Chronicles 22:2, which in the received Hebrew reads "forty and two years old." Both notices end with the same one-year reign and the same mother, Athaliah; only the age differs.

The Kings figure is internally consistent

"Thirty and two years old was he when he began to reign; and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem."

— 2 Kings 8:17 (KJV), source, on Ahaziah's father Jehoram. A father who became king at thirty-two and reigned eight years died at forty; a son of twenty-two fits, a son of forty-two cannot. The Kings account holds together where the Masoretic Chronicles number breaks.

Notes

  • The older account and the Chronicler's source. Kings is conventionally dated to the exilic period (c. 560 BC) and was demonstrably used as a written source by the Chronicler a century and a half later. Its "twenty-two" is therefore not a second, independent sighting but the reading the Chronicler had before him — which is exactly why the Masoretic "forty-two" of Chronicles looks like a later slip rather than a rival tradition. See the summary timeline.
  • Same pattern as the Goliath and Solomon cruxes. A number that differs between the Samuel–Kings tradition and Chronicles, with the fix sought in textual transmission rather than in real history, is the recurring shape of these hard passages — compare Solomon's stalls and who killed Goliath. Here, unusually, the direction is settled: it is the Chronicles number that is corrupt, because the Kings number is the impossible one's only consistent alternative and the versions back it.
