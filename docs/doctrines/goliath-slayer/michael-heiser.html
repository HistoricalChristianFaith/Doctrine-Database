Michael S. Heiser on Who Killed Goliath?

Dates: 2014 ("Clash of the Manuscripts: Goliath & the Hebrew Text of the Old Testament," Bible Study Magazine) · Wikipedia
Summary: The evangelical Hebrew scholar presents the crux to a popular audience as a textbook case of scribal transmission, not contradiction: 2 Samuel 21:19 is a "defective reading," in his words "the result of a scribe's sincere effort to cope with a problematic manuscript," and 1 Chronicles 20:5 preserves the material needed to see what happened. On his account the "contradiction" is resolved once the mechanics of copying are understood, and the Masoretic verse as it stands is in error.

Claims & evidence

Heiser's account of a copying mistake

"Taking these oddities together suggests very strongly that somewhere along the chain of copying the text of 1 Sam 17 one scribe made a mistake in 2 Sam 21:19 that caused a subsequent scribe some trouble."

— Michael S. Heiser, "Who Killed Goliath — David or Elhanan?" (reproducing the Bible Study Magazine essay), source.

Heiser on 2 Samuel 21:19 as a "defective reading"

"The solution to the contradiction between 2 Sam 21:19 and 1 Chr 20:5 is recognizing that 2 Sam 21:19 is a defective reading since it is the result of a scribe's sincere effort to cope with a problematic manuscript."

— Heiser, "Who Killed Goliath," source. The step-by-step reconstruction that accompanies the essay — the "oregim"/weavers dittography, the confusion of the object-marker ʾet with ʾaḥi ("brother of"), and "Bethlehemite" (bet ha-laḥmi) versus the name "Lahmi" — is also given in Gleason Archer's Encyclopedia of Bible Difficulties (1982), p. 179, as cited by Matt Slick (CARM) on the same reproduction page ⚠ unverified (Archer's work not opened).

Notes

  • Direction of correction: toward Chronicles. Heiser sides with the reading that uses 1 Chronicles 20:5 ("Elhanan slew Lahmi the brother of Goliath") to repair the Samuel verse — the direction held by Kennicott and Keil & Delitzsch, and, like them, explains it as a copying accident. This is the opposite of the McCarter line (Samuel primary, Elhanan the original slayer).
  • Error in the Masoretic verse. Heiser describes the Masoretic 2 Samuel 21:19 as defective, writing that "1 Chr 20:5 should be used to correct 2 Sam 21:19."
  • Other readings. Driver (1890) and H. P. Smith (1899) read Chronicles' "Lahmi, the brother of Goliath" as a harmonization of Samuel, and Ozoliņš (2022) takes neither text as the original, holding that both descend from a shared text in which Elhanan killed Goliath's unnamed brother. The readings are set out on the page How did 2 Samuel 21:19 and 1 Chronicles 20:5 come to differ?
