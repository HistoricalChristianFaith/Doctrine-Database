The Shepherd of Hermas on remarriage

Dates: c. 140 · Wikipedia
Summary: The Shepherd is the earliest extant post-apostolic text on the question: the widowed who remarry “do not sin,” but those who stay single “gain greater honour and glory with the Lord.”

Claims & evidence

“No sin in marrying again”; “greater honour” for the unmarried

If a wife or husband die, and the widower or widow marry, does he or she commit sin? There is no sin in marrying again, said he; but if they remain unmarried, they gain greater honour and glory with the Lord; but if they marry, they do not sin.

— Hermas, The Shepherd, Commandment (Mandate) 4.4, ANF vol. 2. The answer is given in the form of a question put to the angelic Shepherd.

Reception and debate
