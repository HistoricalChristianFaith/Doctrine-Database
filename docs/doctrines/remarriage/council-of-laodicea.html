The Council of Laodicea on remarriage

Dates: c. 364 · Wikipedia
Summary: Canon 1 of Laodicea restores communion to those who have “freely and lawfully joined in second marriages” after “a short space” of prayer and fasting.

Claims & evidence

Communion for those “lawfully joined in second marriages” (canon 1)

It is right, according to the ecclesiastical Canon, that the Communion should by indulgence be given to those who have freely and lawfully joined in second marriages, not having previously made a secret marriage; after a short space, which is to be spent by them in prayer and fasting.

— Council of Laodicea, canon 1, NPNF2 vol. 14. The canon describes these marriages as contracted “freely and lawfully” and the communion as given “by indulgence.”

Placement on the timeline

  • Terms of penance for a second marriage are also set by Neocaesarea (canons 3 and 7) and by Basil, who gives a year (“some authorities say two years”).
