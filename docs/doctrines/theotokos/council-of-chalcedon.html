The Council of Chalcedon on Theotokos

Dates: 451 (the fourth ecumenical council, convened by Marcian at Chalcedon) · Wikipedia
Summary: Chalcedon’s Definition of Faith confesses the one Christ born “of the Virgin Mary, the Mother of God according to his manhood,” and receives Cyril’s synodical letters and Leo’s letter to Flavian.

Claims & evidence

“Born… of the Virgin Mary, the Mother of God according to his manhood”

begotten of his Father before the worlds according to his Godhead; but in these last days for us men and for our salvation born [into the world] of the Virgin Mary, the Mother of God according to his manhood.

— The Definition of Faith of the Council of Chalcedon, source.1 The Definition sets the title within its confession of two natures. The one Christ is “begotten of his Father before the worlds according to his Godhead” and “born… of the Virgin Mary, the Mother of God according to his manhood.” It also “receives the synodical letters of the Blessed Cyril… addressed to Nestorius and the Easterns” and adds “the letter of the President of the great and old Rome, the most blessed and holy Archbishop Leo, which was addressed to Archbishop Flavian.”

Notes

  • The Definition states that it observes “the order and every form relating to the faith, which was observed by the holy synod formerly held in Ephesus” (Ephesus, 431).
  • The Percival (NPNF) translation renders the Greek Theotokos here as “the Mother of God”; the bracketed “[into the world]” is the translator’s own insertion.
