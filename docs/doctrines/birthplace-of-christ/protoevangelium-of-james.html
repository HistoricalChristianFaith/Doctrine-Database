The Protoevangelium of James on the birthplace of Christ

Dates: c. 150 · Wikipedia
Summary: The earliest surviving narrative to say where Jesus was born places the birth in a cave outside Bethlehem, reached when Mary’s labour overtakes the family on the road — not in an inn or a stable. This apocryphal infancy gospel is the fountainhead of the cave tradition that Justin, Origen, and the Church of the Nativity would carry forward.

Claims & evidence

Joseph finds a cave and leads Mary into it to give birth

And he found a cave there, and led her into it; and leaving his two sons beside her, he went out to seek a midwife in the district of Bethlehem.

— Protoevangelium of James 17, source. The setting is a journey interrupted: Mary tells Joseph “the child within me presses to come forth,” and there is no time to reach the town. The cave is a roadside shelter, not a lodging refused — the text never mentions an inn or an innkeeper.

A luminous cloud and a blinding light fill the cave at the birth

And they stood in the place of the cave, and behold a luminous cloud overshadowed the cave. … And immediately the cloud disappeared out of the cave, and a great light shone in the cave, so that the eyes could not bear it. And in a little that light gradually decreased, until the infant appeared, and went and took the breast from His mother Mary.

— Protoevangelium of James 19, source. The cave is not incidental scenery: the whole marvel of light and the ensuing midwife-episode (Salome) is staged inside it. The grotto, not a stable, is the memory this second-century text fixes.

Notes

  • Date and authorship: the work is pseudonymous (ascribed to James, the Lord’s brother) and usually dated to the mid-second century (c. 145–150), making it roughly contemporary with — and possibly a source for — Justin Martyr’s cave. Whether the cave is an independent local memory or a literary invention is the substance of the queued cave-authentic-memory argument.
  • The Protoevangelium’s primary aim is Mary’s perpetual virginity (the midwife and Salome test it), so its cave detail is incidental to that program — a point that cuts both ways in weighing whether the cave is remembered or constructed. See also the wiki’s perpetual virginity timeline, where this same work is a key early witness.
