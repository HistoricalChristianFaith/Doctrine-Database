The scenario ledger

Each row is one item of evidence, scored under every scenario into one of four buckets: ✓ fits (positively expected — a win) · ○ neutral (consistent but non-discriminating) · △ costs a date (fits only if a named dating result is wrong) · ✗ unexplained (a residue the scenario cannot absorb even granting its premises). Each cell links the full argument page, which keeps its own neutral assessment; the ledger reports only how that evidence reads conditional on each bundle.
